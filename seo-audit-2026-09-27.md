# Search copy and internal-link audit — 27 September 2026

Scope: existing page text, titles, descriptions and links. No new routes,
database changes, funding classifier, indexing-policy changes or redesign.

## Findings before edits

- The homepage already targeted PhD/postdoc positions in its title and H1,
  but its introduction described the collection process rather than how to
  find a suitable position. Its directory link was inside noscript and there
  were no visible direct links to Biology or Netherlands hubs.
- `/area/biology` and `/country/netherlands` were publicly accessible. They
  already had self-canonicals, indexable HTML and CollectionPage/ItemList
  markup. Their introductions consisted only of listing counts.
- Hub meta descriptions emphasized counts and aggregation from Bluesky rather
  than application information. The Netherlands title omitted the article
  "the". Funding intent was addressed only incidentally in individual posts.
- The checked-in 23 September snapshot contains 5,685 active rows: 1,419 in
  Biology (471 tagged PhD Student; 944 Postdoc) and 418 in the Netherlands
  (228 PhD Student; 188 Postdoc). Tags can overlap. These are snapshot counts,
  not a claim about live availability on the audit date.
- Robots allows crawling and lists the sitemap. Existing noindex rules for
  archive/weak detail pages and deep pagination are outside this change.
- Search Console impressions, clicks, CTR and average position were not
  available. Public page inspection cannot establish Google indexing or rank.

## Changes

- Homepage title: "PhD Positions & Postdoc Jobs Worldwide | PhD Sky".
- Homepage introduction explains subject/country browsing and checking funding.
- Visible homepage links point to the existing directory, Biology hub and
  Netherlands hub with descriptive anchor text.
- Existing hubs receive concise application guidance; Biology has specific
  subject copy and Netherlands addresses the question of finding a funded PhD
  without claiming that every listing is funded.
- Hub titles use "PhD and Postdoc Positions in [subject/country]". Descriptions,
  social metadata, H1 and collection metadata remain consistent.
- Directory page one receives a short introduction and a link to the searchable
  board. Templates preserve this copy on future daily generation.
- Updated copy on 43 existing hubs without changing listings or snapshot dates.

Source transparency is retained: homepage search/social/schema descriptions and
visible homepage, directory and hub introductions identify public Bluesky posts
as the source. The homepage says collection is automatic. These are not presented
as direct employer submissions or a comprehensive vacancy database.

## Measuring the result

After deployment, inspect `/`, `/positions`, `/area/biology` and
`/country/netherlands` in Search Console. Compare equal 28-day periods once
Google has recrawled them, using page and query filters for PhD positions,
biology postdocs and Netherlands PhDs. Monitor impressions, clicks and CTR
alongside position; changing vacancy supply and seasonality can affect results.
No ranking improvement is claimed until measured.

and [link guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/links-crawlable).
