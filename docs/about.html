> About PhD Sky

A free index of public academic opportunities.

What it is

PhD Sky is a free, independent, non-commercial service operated by Eli Eydlin in Israel. It has no advertising, paid listings, sales, or recruiter relationships. Anyone can browse without an account.

An optional account lets you save searches, follow public sources and topics, and receive weekly matching-position emails.

How listings are assembled

  1. Collect. Public academic-position posts are gathered from Bluesky through the AT Protocol and from ScholarshipDB listings.
  2. Classify. Public Bluesky text is screened and labelled by automated language models for position status, academic area, country, and position type. ScholarshipDB positions arrive pre-classified.
  3. Deduplicate. Text similarity and automated review connect reposts while keeping a canonical listing.
  4. Publish. Verified canonical records appear in the feed and crawlable static position and subject pages.

Limits

Automation can be wrong. Listings are not endorsed or verified by PhD Sky, may be outdated, and can link to deceptive third parties. The original source is authoritative. Confirm deadlines, funding, eligibility, supervisor identity, and application instructions with the institution.

Open source and corrections

The pipeline and website source are available on GitHub. To report a classification problem or request correction or removal of your own public post, email eli.eydlin@gmail.com.
