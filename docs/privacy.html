Privacy Notice

Last updated 1 September 2026

Notice when information is requested. Providing account, profile, saved-search, follow, and email-alert information is voluntary and is not required by law. You can browse without an account. If you do not provide an email and authentication information, you cannot create an account; if you do not confirm email consent, no weekly alert is sent. The database controller is Eli Eydlin, an individual in Israel. Contact: eli.eydlin@gmail.com. The purposes, recipients, and your access and correction rights are described below.

Service and commitments

PhD Sky is a free, independent, non-commercial academic position discovery service. It has no sales, paid listings, or advertising. Personal account data is not sold, used for behavioural profiling, or used to train AI models.

Public position text may be processed by automated models to identify whether it is a position and to add subject, country, and position-type labels. That processing concerns public listing content, not account profiles or saved-search ownership.

Information collected

Browsing without an account: Vercel and other network infrastructure receive ordinary request information such as IP address, request time, page, device/browser details, and security signals to deliver and protect the site. Optional analytics is not loaded unless you affirmatively allow it.

Account use:

  • email address, Auth user identifier, account timestamps, and a securely hashed password when email/password is used;
  • optional display name and academic handle;
  • OAuth name/email information if you choose Google or GitHub sign-in;
  • saved-search filters, followed public accounts and topics;
  • weekly-alert consent time/version, delivery setting, processing and notification timestamps, and unsubscribe state; and
  • operational and security records produced by the service providers.

Purposes

  • authenticate you and provide profile correction, saved searches, follows, personalised feeds, export, and deletion;
  • send weekly matching-position emails only after explicit confirmation and honour unsubscribe choices;
  • operate, secure, debug, prevent abuse, and maintain the service;
  • respond to privacy, correction, deletion, and listing-removal requests; and
  • measure aggregate site use only if optional analytics consent is given.

Information is not used for materially different purposes without an appropriate notice and, where required, consent.

Saved searches and weekly email

Saving a search does not turn on email. A separate confirmation shows the filters, current account email, and weekly frequency. Successful no-match scans advance a processing watermark so old listings are not repeatedly examined. Failed sends advance neither the processing nor notification watermark.

Every email has a human confirmation page for “Unsubscribe this alert” and “Stop all weekly emails”, plus the standards-based one-click headers used by compatible mailbox providers. A GET request never unsubscribes you. Manage alerts at Saved searches.

Cookies and device storage

  • phdsky_consent is the necessary first-party cookie used by the consent tool to remember your choice for up to 182 days.
  • A Supabase Auth session is stored in browser storage when you sign in, so the requested account remains available. It is removed on sign-out or expiry.
  • _ga and _ga_* may be set by Google Analytics only after affirmative analytics consent, generally for up to 24 months. Withdrawing consent sends a denial signal and deletes accessible GA cookies.
  • Vercel Analytics and Google Analytics scripts are not requested before consent. The fonts, Supabase browser library, and consent library are hosted by PhD Sky, so Google Fonts and jsDelivr do not receive ordinary page-load requests.

Analytics refusal or withdrawal does not affect access to the service. Change the choice at any time through Cookie settings.

Recipients and international processing

Information is disclosed only as needed to the following categories of recipients. These providers may process information in Israel, the United States, the European Economic Area, or other countries where they or their sub-processors operate:

  • Supabase: authentication, profiles, database, saved searches, follows, and alert state;
  • Vercel: hosting, network/security logs, serverless unsubscribe endpoint, and optional analytics after consent;
  • Resend: recipient address, subject, message content, headers, and delivery events for requested weekly email;
  • Google or GitHub only when you choose that sign-in provider; Google Analytics only after analytics consent;
  • NVIDIA and Mistral for automated classification of public listing text, not account ownership or account-profile data; and
  • authorities or professional advisers when disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights and security.

Using an international online service necessarily involves processing outside Israel. Appropriate contractual, technical, and organisational safeguards are used where applicable, but foreign legal protections can differ from Israeli law.

Retention and deletion

  • Profile, follows, topics, and saved searches remain while the account exists and are deleted through the account-deletion cascade.
  • Alert consent and unsubscribe state remain with the saved search until that search or the account is deleted. Provider delivery/security records follow the provider’s documented retention obligations.
  • Optional analytics follows the provider setting, up to the periods described above, unless consent is withdrawn and accessible cookies are deleted sooner.
  • Infrastructure security logs are retained only according to operational and provider retention schedules.
  • Public listing records are retained for discovery and deduplication until removed, superseded, or no longer needed. Authors and rights holders may request removal.

Backups and provider logs may take a limited period to expire after deletion where technically necessary or legally required.

Access, correction, export, deletion, and objection

Under Israel’s Privacy Protection Law, a person may request access to personal information about them in a database and request correction or deletion where information is incorrect, incomplete, unclear, or out of date, subject to the law’s conditions and exceptions. Depending on where you live, additional rights such as portability, restriction, objection, or withdrawal of consent may apply.

The Account & privacy view supports profile correction, JSON export, cookie settings, and typed-confirmation deletion. You can also email eli.eydlin@gmail.com. Identity may be verified before fulfilling a request. Withdrawing consent does not make earlier lawful processing unlawful.

Public listings and removal

Listings are drawn from public sources and link back to them. If you authored a listing or represent a rights holder and want it corrected or removed, email eli.eydlin@gmail.com with the source URL and request.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be posted here with an updated date. Privacy questions, complaints, access/correction requests, and removal requests should be sent to Eli Eydlin at eli.eydlin@gmail.com.
